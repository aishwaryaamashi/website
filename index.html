Since 8th Standard 🎒 🚶‍♀️

Our never-ending walks

We've been walking to school together since eighth standard. Same roads, same nonsense, and somehow we never ran out of things to talk about. Those walks turned into a whole never-ending story and remain one of my absolute favourite memories of us.

📌
Package Deal 👯‍♀️ ❓

"Where is she?"

When one of us was absent, everyone would instantly ask the other about her. We became such a familiar pair that people noticed the second one of us wasn't around. Like... why are you asking me? (Obviously because we're inseparable!)

📌
Partners in Crime 🚨 🪑

The seat-changing incidents

Teachers literally used to change our seats because we talked so much together! Every time they separated us, it felt like they were trying to break up a criminal syndicate. And honestly? We still found ways to yap across the room.

📌
24/7 Hotlines 📱 🗣️

Professional yap sessions

Random reels, making videos at your house, unplanned late-night video calls, and talking about absolutely anything under the sun. No script, no reason, just pure unfiltered best friend conversation.

📌
Second Home 🏠 🛋️

Our unofficial second home

Going to your house just to chill, spend time together, and do absolutely nothing particularly important. Just existing there like I practically live there—one of the most comforting feelings ever.

📌
Honors Degree ☕ 🍵

The gossip department

Our iconic sessions of bitching about people together. At this point, we could give masterclasses and publish textbooks on our analysis. Unmatched therapy sessions honestly.

📌
Festive Chaos ✨ 🪔

Pandal-hopping adventures

Our memories of visiting pandals together—the festive atmosphere, the crowds, the food, the Ganesh Chaturthi outings, and our usual chaos navigating through it all together.

📌
Unintentional Heroes 🦠 🏖️

The accidental class holiday

The time both of us got COVID and the entire class ended up getting an unexpected holiday because of us! Being sick was definitely not fun, but being responsible for the whole class getting a day off is definitely hall-of-fame material.
